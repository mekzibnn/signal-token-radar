MODEL NOTES

Every estimate has a trail.

Implied value = exp(a + b × ln(TVL)) — a log-log size curve fitted on the recorded-token peers. The fitted a and b are shown in the validation note above and in the "How a value is built" panel on the board.

The low–high band on each row is a bootstrap interval: 200 resamples of the recorded-token peers, refit, 5th–95th percentile. It is a measured stability band, not an assumed stress band. TVL is not revenue; market cap is not fully diluted value. No supply, distribution, revenue or launch probability is available in the snapshot.

Farm plans are netted per hands-on hour on fixed terms: a $1,000 capital slice, 30 days, ~2 hands-on hours, 6 transactions (Ethereum $12 each, other chains $1.50 each), opportunity cost at ~5% APY. The reward pool is 5% of the low-band implied value, shared by one active farmer per $10,000 of TVL. Verdict: farm if net per hour > $25, marginal (thin) if > $0, skip otherwise. Each row also shows its break-even farmer count and break-even odds. No reward is assumed until a protocol publishes terms. Asset support, access, points, eligibility and live transaction fees require independent verification. Unsupported or restricted products are monitor-only.
