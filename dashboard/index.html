MODEL NOTES

Every estimate has a trail.

Implied value = exp(a + b × ln(TVL)) — a log-log size curve fitted on the recorded-token peers. The a and b values are shown in the validation note above.

The v1 category-median multiple was kept as a baseline and lost on both tests. 7D growth, listing age and chain count were tested as extra features and removed — each made the out-of-sample and/or blind error worse. No bias correction: the two tests disagree on the bias, so a correction fitted on one would not survive the other.

The low–high band on each row is a bootstrap interval: 200 resamples of the recorded-token peers, refit, 5th–95th percentile. It is a measured stability band, not an assumed stress band. TVL is not revenue; market cap is not fully diluted value. No supply, distribution, revenue or launch probability is available in the snapshot.

Farm plans are netted to the dollar: scenario reward (farmers share 5% of the low-band implied value, one active farmer is 1 of ~5,000 sharing it) minus gas for 6 transactions and 30 days of capital opportunity cost. Verdict: farm if net > $25, thin if net > $0, skip otherwise. The 1-in-5,000 share is an explicit assumption; change it and the verdicts move. No reward is assumed until a protocol publishes terms. Asset support, access, points, eligibility and live transaction fees require independent verification. Unsupported or restricted products are monitor-only.
