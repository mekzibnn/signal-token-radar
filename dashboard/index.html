MODEL NOTES

Every estimate has a trail.

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier.

The peer multiple is the median recorded market-cap/TVL ratio in the protocol’s category, clipped to 0.05–2.0, then shrunk toward the global peer median with eight prior observations. Thin peer groups therefore rely more on the global baseline.

Growth = 1 + 0.35 × tanh(7D TVL change / 35). Missing growth uses zero. Maturity = 0.65 + 0.35 × (1 − exp(−listing age / 540)); missing age uses 365 days. Listing date is when tracking began and does not establish launch date or continuous absence of a token.

The displayed 0.5×–1.7× range is an assumption stress band, not a statistical confidence interval. TVL is not revenue; market cap is not fully diluted value. No supply, distribution, revenue or launch probability is available in the snapshot.

Farm plans are category-based research templates with illustrative budgets. Asset support, access, points, eligibility and live transaction fees require independent verification. Unsupported or restricted products are monitor-only. No reward is assumed.
