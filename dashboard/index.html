MODEL NOTES

Every estimate has a trail.

Implied value = exp(a + b × ln(TVL)) × family × growth × maturity — a log-log size curve fitted on the recorded-token peers, times the family factor (the category's shrunken residual, k=5), 7-day growth 1 + 0.35 × tanh(change_7d / 35) and listing age 0.65 + 0.35 × (1 − exp(−age_days / 540)): the rubric's three inputs plus the family factor. The fitted a and b are shown above and in the "How a value is built" panel; each row's multipliers are shown in its detail view.

The low–high band on each row is a bootstrap interval: 200 resamples of the recorded-token peers, refit, 5th–95th percentile. Each band maps to a rank range (Rank range column); every row's rank lies inside its range. TVL is not revenue; market cap is not fully diluted value. No supply, distribution, revenue or launch probability is available in the snapshot.

Farm plans are netted per hands-on hour on fixed terms: a $5,000 capital slice, 30 days, ~2 hands-on hours, 6 transactions (Ethereum $12 each, other chains $1.50 each), opportunity cost at ~5% APY. Slice if a token launches = 10% airdrop share × implied value × ($5,000 deposit / TVL); break-even odds = total cost / slice. Verdict: farm if net per hour > $25 and token odds clear the odds gate, marginal (thin) if > $0, skip otherwise. Each row shows its slice, total cost, break-even odds and net per hour. No reward is assumed until a protocol publishes terms. Asset support, access, points, eligibility and live fees require independent verification. Unsupported or restricted products are monitor-only.
